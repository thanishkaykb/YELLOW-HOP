# 🌻 Yellow Hop

A cute and minimal endless jumping game inspired by the classic Chrome Dinosaur Game!

Jump over beautiful sunflowers, collect points, and challenge yourself to beat your highest score.

## 🎮 How to Play

- Press **SPACE** or **↑ Arrow** to jump.
- You can also click or tap to jump.
- Avoid colliding with the sunflowers.
- Earn **10 points** for every successfully cleared sunflower.
- Try to beat your highest score!

## ✨ Features

- Minimal sunflower-themed design.
- Smooth jumping gameplay.
- Endless obstacle generation.
- Real-time score tracking.
- Best score saved using Local Storage.
- Simple and responsive controls.
